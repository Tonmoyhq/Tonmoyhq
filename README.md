# Mohibbul Haque, MBBS, MPH

### MPhil Candidate in Preventive & Social Medicine · Medical Doctor · Medical Epidemiologist · Public Health Researcher

I am a medical doctor and public-health researcher based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. My work focuses on epidemiology, preventive medicine, environmental and occupational health, health systems, and evidence-informed public health research.

## MPhil Thesis

**Association Between Chemical Exposure and Leukaemia: A Case-Control Study**

My MPhil research examines the association between chemical exposure and leukaemia using a case-control study design. This work contributes to environmental and occupational epidemiology, cancer prevention, and evidence-based public-health policy.

# Mohibbul Haque, MBBS, MPH

### MPhil Candidate in Preventive & Social Medicine · Medical Doctor · Medical Epidemiologist · Environmental & Occupational Health Researcher · Cancer Epidemiology

I am a medical doctor and MPhil researcher in Preventive and Social Medicine based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. My research focuses on environmental and occupational epidemiology, cancer prevention, antimicrobial resistance, and evidence-informed public health practice.

My MPhil thesis investigates the association between chemical exposure and leukaemia through a case-control study. I am interested in applying rigorous epidemiological methods and reproducible research practices to improve population health.

## MPhil Thesis

**Association Between Chemical Exposure and Leukaemia: A Case-Control Study**

My MPhil research examines the association between chemical exposure and leukaemia using a case-control study design. This work contributes to environmental and occupational epidemiology, cancer prevention, and evidence-based public-health policy.
